## Custom avatar options / cost (from LiveAvatar docs)
- **Stock**: free to use (no avatar slot); still consumes session credits (LITE=1 credit/min).
- **Custom image avatar**: 1 photo; no consent video; **no voice clone** (voice comes from ElevenLabs in LITE); requires **paid avatar slot add-on**.
- **Custom video avatar**: ~2 min footage + consent recording; auto voice clone; requires **paid avatar slot add-on**.
- Slot pricing is a LiveAvatar subscription add-on — exact USD not in public docs; check https://app.liveavatar.com billing. Plans: Free $0/10 credits, Essential $99/1,100, Business $475/6,000.
